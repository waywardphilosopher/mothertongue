Next month, we'll be up north in Taipei, running a two-day workshop at National Taiwan Normal University. The workshop is titled 祖傳語言與跨文化的翻譯與對話: 翻譯與創意寫作工作坊 *Heritage Languages in Translation & Cross-Cultural Dialogue: translation & creative writing workshop*. There will be three of us from the Tâigael team: editor Dr. Will Buckingham, and writers Kiú-kiong and Naomi Sím, for an action-packed two days of translation, talks, discussion, poa̍h-poe 跋杯 divination, and lots more. 

Here's the poster. The workshop is open to all interested translators!
