We're delighted to announce that we're now being distributed by [BookSource](https://www.booksource.net), one of the UK's leading book distributors. Earlier this year, we joined Publishing Scotland, and BookSource is Publishing Scotland's distribution arm, dedicated to getting books into the hands of readers.

This means that if you are a bookshop, it is even easier to get hold of our books. And if you are in Taiwan, then we're excited to announce a new partnership with [書林書店 / Bookman Books](https://www.bookman.com.tw), who are printing our books in Taiwan, and distributing our books across the country.

We're excited by these new partnerships that will bring *Tâigael* to even wider audiences.
